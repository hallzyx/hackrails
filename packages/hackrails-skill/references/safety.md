# Safety Boundaries

## Credentials

- A participant bearer token is scoped to its assigned team and event; it is not a wallet credential.
- Never request, store, expose, or log organizer wallet credentials, Hedera private keys, seed phrases, or bearer tokens.
- Keep proprietary repository content out of tool inputs unless the tool request explicitly requires a safe, minimal public reference.

## Sponsored Access

- Premium calls are paid by the organizer under event policy, not by participants.
- A policy rejection creates neither provider work nor payment. Preserve the message and direct the participant to the organizer; do not attempt a bypass.

## Evidence Boundary

Settlement fields prove a call's payment state. They do not override event rules, reveal judge preferences, or guarantee an outcome.
