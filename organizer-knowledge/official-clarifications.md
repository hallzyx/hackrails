# Official Clarifications — Hedera x402 Builder Sprint

## Verified: 2026-07-24

## Q: Can we use mainnet for the demo?
**A:** No. Mainnet transactions are not accepted as evidence. Testnet only.

## Q: Is a license required in the repository?
**A:** A license is recommended but not mandatory for submission. However, open-source is a requirement, and the absence of a license may create ambiguity about usage rights.

## Q: Can the demo video be longer than 5 minutes if it shows more detail?
**A:** No. Videos exceeding 5 minutes risk auto-disqualification. If more detail is needed, submit supplementary documentation, not a longer video.

## Q: Do we need to show the initial HTTP 402 response in the video?
**A:** Yes. The video must show the complete flow including the initial 402 response, the payment payload, the retry, and the final resource delivery.

## Q: Is USDC required or can we use HBAR?
**A:** Both HBAR and USDC are valid settlement assets on Hedera Testnet for this bounty.

## Q: Can teams have more than one payer wallet?
**A:** One nominated payer per team handles prize splitting. For the demo, a single payer transaction is sufficient evidence.

## Q: Are reference architectures mandatory?
**A:** No. Reference architectures (agent-per-query, pay-to-read marketplace) are starting points, not requirements. Projects that diverge from these are encouraged.

## Q: Can we use a facilitator other than the recommended one?
**A:** Yes, as long as the settlement is verifiable on Hedera Testnet and the x402 protocol is followed.
